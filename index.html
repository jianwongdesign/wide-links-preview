Something else on your mind? Call +65 6638 8128 or WhatsApp us. We'd be glad to talk it through.

How do I get a price?

Everything is priced on enquiry. Call us or send the enquiry form with your space and needs. We'll quote, then confirm the quote after measuring on site.

Do I need exact measurements to enquire?

No. Rough measurements are enough to start. We measure on site before the quote is confirmed.

Do you install what you supply?

Yes. Our own installers deliver and fit everything. We don't subcontract the installation.

Can you make custom furniture?

Yes. We build counters, cabinets and one-offs to your drawings, or ours.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve offices, schools and studios island-wide in Singapore. Site visits are by appointment.
