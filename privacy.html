Privacy policy

Last updated July 2026

1. Who we are

Wide-Links Marketing Services Pte Ltd ("Wide-Links", "we") supplies and installs office furniture and flooring in Singapore. This policy explains how we handle personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

2. What we collect

When you enquire, by form, phone or email, we collect your name, company or school, contact details, and the details of your space and requirements. We do not collect payment information through this website.

3. How we use it

We use your data to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange site visits and deliveries, and keep records of completed work. We may contact you about your ongoing or past orders. We do not send marketing you have not asked for.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with delivery and installation partners where needed to fulfil your order, and where required by law.

5. Retention & security

Enquiry and project records are kept for as long as needed for business and legal purposes, then disposed of securely. Access is limited to staff who need it.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time. Contact us at +65 6638 8128 or through the enquiry page.
